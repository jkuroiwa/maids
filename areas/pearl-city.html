Cleaning around Pearl Harbor

Pearl City and Aiea sit right next to Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam, and that shapes the work we do here.

A large share of it is move-out cleaning. PCS orders come with a hard date, an inspection, and either a deposit or a housing clearance riding on how the place looks when you hand over the keys. That is a different job from a regular tidy-up: it is an empty house, every surface, and a standard someone else is going to check. We clean to that standard, and we can work to your date.

The rest is steady recurring service for families where everyone works. The neighborhoods here run from single-family homes up the hillside to townhomes and condos closer to the highway, so there is no one typical job — which is why we price on your actual square footage rather than a flat rate.

What we do most in Pearl City & Aiea

  • Move-out cleaning — for PCS moves, end of lease, and sales
  • Recurring cleaning — weekly, every two weeks, or every four weeks
  • Deep cleaning — the full 22-step Healthy Touch clean before guests or after a busy stretch

We have cleaned homes on this side of the island for over 30 years. Teams are trained, insured and bonded, and every clean carries our guarantee — if any area is not right, call within 24 hours and we will re-clean it free.
