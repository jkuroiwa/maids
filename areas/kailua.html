Cleaning windward homes

Kailua homes are not town condos. They are mostly single-family houses with more square footage, more windows, and a lot more outside coming inside.

Living this close to the water changes the job. Trade winds carry salt air inland, and it settles on window glass, sliding door tracks, lanai railings and fixtures. Sand comes home from Kailua and Lanikai beaches on feet and towels and works its way into carpet and along baseboards. The windward side also holds humidity, so mildew turns up on bathroom grout and window sills faster than it does on the other side of the Pali.

Those are the places our teams pay extra attention to here — tracks and sills, glass, grout, and the floors right inside the doors.

What we do most in Kailua

  • Recurring cleaning — every two or four weeks is the most common choice for Kailua families
  • Deep cleaning — our full 22-step Healthy Touch clean, windows included
  • Move-out cleaning — for rentals and sales across the windward side

Kailua and Kaneohe are a regular part of our route, not a special trip. We have cleaned windward homes for over 30 years, every team is trained and insured, and every clean is backed by our guarantee: if you are not happy with an area, call within 24 hours and we will re-clean it free.
